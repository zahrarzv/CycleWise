# VISION STATEMENT - CYCLEWISE

**CycleWise** is a period-tracking application designed for people who experience menstrual cycles and want to better understand their patterns and plan around different cycle phases. It provides a centralized and private space to log period dates and key health details such as flow intensity, symptoms, and notes, helping to turn scattered or forgotten information into a consistent and reliable record over time.

As cycle data is collected, CycleWise organizes this information into clear and accessible insights. It provides predictions such as the next expected period start date, average cycle length, and current cycle phase, allowing people to better anticipate and understand their bodies. To further support daily use, CycleWise offers simple, phase-specific tips and allows customized notifications for upcoming predicted periods. A clear and intuitive navigation structure ensures that people can easily move between logging, viewing, and understanding their data without confusion.

Menstrual changes—such as missed periods, unusually painful cramps, heavier bleeding, or large clots—can sometimes signal underlying health issues such as hormonal imbalances, polycystic ovary syndrome (PCOS), endometriosis, or thyroid disorders, and should not be automatically dismissed as “normal.” Since menstrual health remains under-researched and under-discussed, people who experience periods are too often misdiagnosed or left without adequate support, which can delay care and, in serious cases, lead to preventable harm.

Many people also experience symptoms or irregularities that are difficult to track over time or are often overlooked. CycleWise addresses this by making patterns more visible and easier to understand. By organizing cycle data and presenting clear predictions and phase-based insights, it supports better awareness of personal patterns. This allows people to plan daily activities, manage symptoms more effectively, and recognize changes that may be worth discussing with a healthcare professional.

The success of CycleWise is primarily measured by its ability to provide meaningful and reliable cycle predictions based on logged data. CycleWise aims to predict the next cycle start date within ±2–3 days for people with at least three recent and consistently logged cycles in at least 80% of test cases. This reflects the core value of the application in delivering useful, data-driven insights that support planning, symptom awareness, and personal health understanding beyond simple tracking.
